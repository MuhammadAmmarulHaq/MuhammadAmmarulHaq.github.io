This is the first post, so here is the plan.

## what goes here

- **machine writeups** from hack the box and tryhackme, written after the box retires
- **ctf writeups** for challenges i actually solved, including the dead ends
- **tooling notes** for nmap, burp, ffuf, bloodhound, impacket and friends
- **mistakes** i made, because those were the expensive part

## what does not go here

No live targets. No client data. No weaponised exploits against systems i do not own.
Everything published is from legal labs, public challenges, or my own vms.

## how to follow

Every post is on the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}), and the
[rss feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}) works in any reader.

## how this site is built

Plain text, jekyll, github pages. No trackers, no newsletter popup, no cookie banner.
The whole site is a repo, and the source of every writeup is a markdown file.
